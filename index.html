The real problems

Nine reasons most cleaning company websites lose to the local 3-pack

Cleaning is sold on trust (you are in their home) and reliability (you show up consistently). Most cleaning sites we audit are leaking qualified bookings in ways the owner hasn't noticed yet.

No instant quote homeowners have to call or fill a form

Cleaning is a low-consideration purchase. Homeowners want a price in 30 seconds. Sites with instant-quote tools convert 3–4x better than call-for-quote competitors.

Recurring plan pricing hidden behind Contact us

Weekly, biweekly, and monthly are the highest-LTV services in cleaning. Hiding pricing kills the comparison-shopper who wants to budget upfront.

No bonded-and-insured trust signals in the header

Homeowners letting strangers into their homes look for bonded-and-insured language first. Buried in a footer or missing means prospects assume you are not.

No cleaner profiles or team visibility

Background-checked, English-speaking, trained on green products—these are silent objections. Cleaner profile photos with first names and certifications shortcut the trust gap.

Generic Services page where service-specific pages should live

Move-out cleaning, post-construction, deep cleaning, Airbnb turnover—each is a separate search intent with different urgency. One services page collapses all of it.

Online booking flow takes 8 taps on mobile

Average cleaning prospect arrives on mobile, scrolls one page, decides in under 30 seconds. Booking flows with more than 2 taps lose half the prospects.

No service-area clarity – wasting dispatch on out-of-zone leads

Specific ZIP codes and a service-area map filter out-of-zone leads and prevent the dispatch-co-cancel waste that kills margin.

No eco-friendly or green product positioning

25-35% of cleaning prospects consist of young children, pet owners, chemical-sensitive adults filter on green products. Sites without EPA Safer Choice language lose this segment.

Reviews live on Google but not on the site

You have 4.9 stars and 250 Google reviews – but your homepage shows zero. Prospects will not open a new tab to verify; they decide based on what is in front of them.

Instant quote calculator

Square footage + bedroom count + bathroom count + service type + frequency -> instant price. Routes into Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ZenMaid with proper field mapping.

Recurring plan comparison

Weekly, biweekly, monthly side-by-side with transparent pricing and what is included. Filters one-time shoppers, qualifies recurring prospects who become 18-36 month LTV.

Bonded-and-insured badges in the header

Bonding amount, liability insurance carrier, workers comp status. Trust signals visible where prospects actually look.

Team profile page with cleaner photos

First names, years with company, training certifications (IICRC, ARCSI), background-check disclosure. Anxious customers see real people, not stock photos.

Eco-friendly product messaging

EPA Safer Choice certification, plant-based product lines, pet-safe and child-safe formulations. Wins the 25-35% filtering on green products.

Service-specific landing pages

Move-out, post-construction, Airbnb turnover, deep cleaning, recurring residential – each its own page with appropriate urgency and pricing structure.

Service type -> date/time -> contact info. Three screens, three taps, into your scheduling platform.

Live review aggregation from Google and Yelp

Real reviews pulled live, embedded on homepage and service pages. Schema-marked for rich snippets.

Service area map with ZIP coverage
